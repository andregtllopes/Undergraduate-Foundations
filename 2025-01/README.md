# Semester 2025/01: Technical Peak and Strategic Consolidation

This semester represented the most demanding period of my academic trajectory, with a record workload of nine professional-level courses. It marks the definitive integration of Aerospace Engineering with Computer Science, balancing high-pressure academic performance with the beginning of a Minor in CS.

## 📌 Overview

The focus of this period was the mastery of physical systems modeling, power electronics, and advanced software architecture. This semester acted as a technical bridge, where the "First-Principles" of engineering were translated into complex algorithmic implementations and automated experimental pipelines.

## 🛠️ Key Academic Focus

- **Programming & Software Development I (DCC203):** Mastery of the C language and structured programming, focusing on memory management and algorithms.
- **Control Theory & Circuits (EMA028 / ELE065):** Study of linear system stability, frequency response, and controller design using the Ziegler-Nichols method.
- **Industrial Electronics (EMA248):** Practical application of power semiconductors and operational amplifiers in instrumentation and signal processing.
- **Thermodynamics (EMA087):** Mastery of energy conservation laws applied to control volumes, including the study of Otto, Diesel, and Brayton cycles.
- **Solid Mechanics & Dynamics (EES003 / EMA252):** Consolidation of stress/strain analysis in beams and 3D rigid body dynamics.

## 🚀 Noteworthy Academic Projects

### 🕹️ Space Invaders Clone (PDS1)
A full-scale implementation of the classic arcade game developed in C using the Allegro 5 library.

- **Technical Highlights:** Implementation of a robust game loop, real-time event handling, and custom structs for entity state management.
- **Algorithms:** Developed synchronized collective movement for alien hordes and area-intersection collision detection.
- **Outcome:** Achieved a maximum score with bonuses for a live-code logic modification during the technical defense.

### 🧪 Experimental Fluid Engineering (Fluid Lab)
A shift from manual workflows to Python-based scientific pipelines (NumPy, Pandas, CoolProp) for processing experimental data.

- **Automation:** Developed iterative solvers for Reynolds and Drag Coefficient ($C_d$) coupling and validated Rateux laws in blowers using 30Hz operational data.
- **Final Project (Hoppler Viscometer):** Designed a functional falling-ball viscometer under severe geometric constraints.
  - **Constraint-Based Design:** Utilized numerical simulations to model Faxén’s wall effect corrections, resulting in a final measurement uncertainty of only 1.57%, meeting commercial precision standards.

## 🧠 Perspective

Completing this semester required a high level of prioritization and career maturity. The success in both the aerospace core and the computer science projects confirmed my decision to pursue the Minor in Computer Science, solidifying programming as a central pillar of my professional identity.
